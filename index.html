LOW-END PC

SONAL MTA OPTIMIZER

Simple setup checklist for smoother MTA: San Andreas gameplay. Offline dashboard; it does not change settings by itself.

In-game graphics

  • Try 1024×768 or 1280×720.
  • Set visual effects to Low.
  • Disable anti-aliasing and heavy shaders.
  • Reduce draw distance if FPS is low.

Windows prep

  • Close apps you recognize and do not need.
  • Keep the PC ventilated.
  • Use official graphics-driver updates.
  • Try sonal.cmd only if you want the High Performance power plan.

MTA resource

  • Put client.lua and meta.xml in a resource folder.
  • Start it only on a server you own/manage or with admin permission.
  • Type /sonal to toggle the visual preset.

Safety & expectations

  • FPS gains depend on your hardware and the server.
  • Lower view distance may hide distant scenery.
  • No registry edits, injectors, or security disabling.
